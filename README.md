# Two Clocks

A private journal for two people and their two rhythms.

Two Clocks helps two people say out loud the practical things that usually go unsaid: what they are each looking for, how fast they like things to move, how much space they need, how they reply, and how they handle tension. It is for relationships kept on purpose.

Live at [twoclocks.app](https://twoclocks.app/).

## Five ways in

- **Before you meet.** For two people who are talking but haven't met. Seventeen questions about what you are each looking for, from the long-term picture to affection, timing and intimacy, each with a live illustration and a short note on why it matters. One answer, about what draws you, never leaves your device.
- **The Blueprint.** Twelve questions across four pillars: bandwidth, contact, conflict and intent. Compared side by side, with an agreement to adopt for each difference.
- **Unopened Rooms.** A deck of 32 questions, plus your own, for the people you already think you know.
- **Internal Weather.** A ten-second check-in for hectic weeks: how it is inside, how much energy is left, and what would actually help.
- **Seasonal Recalibration.** Dated snapshots of your Blueprint, so you can see how you have each changed.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, no logins, no server and no analytics.

- Answers travel only inside the links people choose to send. They sit after the `#` in the address, which browsers never send to a server.
- Progress is saved in the browser's local storage on your own device.
- You can export or import a single "dossier" file for an offline copy.
- The app asks nothing about age, gender or orientation.

## How it's built

One self-contained file, `index.html`, with all HTML, CSS and JavaScript inline. There are no frameworks, external fonts, CDNs or build steps. It works offline once loaded.

To run it, open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Hosting

Any static host works: GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages. Serve `index.html` from the root. `.app` domains require HTTPS, which these hosts provide automatically.
